It selects "#-group .inline-related" (any element), clones "#-empty" to add a row, and appends its JS remove link to each new row's FIRST child -- hence the card header comes first. Everything the formset machinery needs is still emitted: the js-inline-admin-formset wrapper with its data-inline-formset payload, the management form, pk/fk hidden inputs, the empty-form template, the DELETE checkbox for saved rows, and every error list.
